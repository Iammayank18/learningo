🚫 Common HLD Interview Anti-Patterns
✗
Jumping to solution — Drawing architecture before clarifying requirements. Always spend 5 min on requirements first.
✗
Over-engineering — Proposing Kafka + microservices + Kubernetes for a simple CRUD app. Match complexity to scale.
✗
No trade-offs — Saying "I'd use Redis" without explaining why, and what the downside is. Every choice has a cost.
✗
Ignoring failures — Designing only the happy path. Interviewers always ask "what if X fails?"
✗
No numbers — Not doing back-of-envelope. You can't justify your DB choice without knowing the QPS.
✗
Monologue — Talking for 10 minutes without checking in with the interviewer. Design is a conversation, not a lecture.
✗
Vague components — Drawing a box labelled "Cache" without saying what type, what TTL, what eviction policy, why.
